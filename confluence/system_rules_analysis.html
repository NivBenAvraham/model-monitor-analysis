Actionable Recommendations
What can be implemented based on this analysis.
1 — Use Pred as a "safe lane"

When Pred says valid, trust it (P=0.941 on test, 1 FP). This becomes a guaranteed auto-approve channel with very low risk. Low recall (20%) means it won't catch everything, but when it fires it's almost always right.

2 — Use Pred OR Temperature as the main system

Combine Pred and Temperature with OR logic to get Precision=0.872, Recall=0.474. This catches 3× more valid cases than Pred alone while still rejecting most FPs. Replaces both single rules with one combined signal.

3 — Pred AND Temperature as an ultra-safe channel

When both rules independently agree on valid, Precision=1.0 (0 FP on train). Low recall (0.057) but zero risk. Useful as a fully automated approval tier, e.g., skip manual review entirely for these cases.

4 — Deprioritise Triage as a standalone signal

Triage alone has 50 FPs on train (Precision=0.679). It should only be used in combination — either AND with another rule to tighten it, or to understand coverage gaps. Never fire an auto-approval on Triage alone.

5 — Consider a tiered approval system

Tier 1 (auto-approve, 0 FP): Pred AND Temperature agrees → valid
Tier 2 (auto-approve, ~1 FP): Pred agrees → valid
Tier 3 (high confidence): Pred OR Temperature → valid
Tier 4 (manual review): Only Triage or no signal → review

6 — Investigate top discriminating metrics

clipping_diff, clip_diff_p90, and clip_diff_mean are the strongest individual separators (discrimination 0.44–0.57, precision 0.86–0.91). These are strong candidates to either enhance the Pred rule threshold or build a new supplementary metric check.
